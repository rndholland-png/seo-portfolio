Topical Authority & Content Merging

Enterprise Content Decay Recovery

Pivoting a multi-region home services brand from legacy keyword targeting to AI Overview citation networks.

The 6-Year Growth Narrative

Over a six-year partnership, I helped scale a regional metal roofing startup (represented here as Summit Metal Systems) into a multi-market industry leader. As the brand expanded across Portland, Spokane, and four distinct markets in the Greater Seattle area, the challenge shifted from basic local exposure to defending enterprise search equity.

Faced with natural historical content decay and a rapidly changing SERP landscape, I executed a **comprehensive Content Audit, Decay Analysis, and Semantic Mapping project**. The goal: reclaim lost traffic and restructure informational assets to capture conversational AI search queries.

The Legacy Problem

  • Content Decay: Older blog posts targeting single, hyper-specific queries slowly losing search volume and ranking power.
  • Fragmented Authority: Separate short-form articles covering overlapping concepts, leading to self-cannibalization.
  • AI Exclusion: Thin, non-structured content failing to provide the density required for LLM extraction and AI Overview citations.

The Modern Strategy

  • Content Merging: Consolidating weak, decaying articles into definitive, long-form master resources.
  • Multi-Intent Clustering: Mapping topics to address several high-value keywords and intent layers in a single asset.
  • LLM Compatibility: Deploying structured data, high-density informational tables, and explicit summaries to earn AI citations.

The Modern SEO Paradigm

This blueprint illustrates why legacy "volume-chasing" content plans fail over time. By auditing performance trends over several years, recognizing early signs of decay, and converting outdated articles into highly extractable topic hubs, we achieved two goals: defending local search equity across multiple geographic expansions and securing prime real estate in modern AI engine answers.
