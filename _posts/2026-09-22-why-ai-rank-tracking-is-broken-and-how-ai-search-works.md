If you've been watching the debate around AI search, you've likely noticed a lot of skepticism around tools claiming they can "track your rankings" in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews.

The skepticism is entirely justified. Legacy SEO tracking tools were built for a world of static links and fixed keywords. But AI doesn't work like a standard search engine:

- **AI answers change constantly:** Ask ChatGPT the same question three times, and you'll get three slightly different answers, brand lists, or phrasing.
- **Prompt volume is a black box:** Anyone claiming they know the exact "search volume" for a conversational AI prompt is making up math.
- **Search is personalized:** Chat history, memory, and custom instructions change what every individual user sees.

Most "AI rank trackers" on the market today are applying old rules to a completely new game. They are selling vanity metrics and guesswork.

## From Static Rankings to Recommendation Probability

In traditional search, Google scanned a page, matched keywords, counted links, and assigned a static rank.

AI search doesn't work that way. When a potential customer asks ChatGPT or Perplexity for a recommendation — like *"Who is the best commercial metal roofing contractor in Seattle?"* — the AI synthesizes a fresh answer on the spot using its internal knowledge graph and web sources.

Because the AI's response changes slightly every time, you can't measure AI search using a single "Rank #1" metric. Instead, you have to measure **probability**.

If an AI engine recommends your brand in 80 out of 100 tests across different ways a customer might ask for help, your business has a dominant presence. You aren't chasing a single ranking; you are building high visibility across the entire consideration set.
