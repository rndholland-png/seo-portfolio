If you think Local SEO is just about the Map Pack, think again.

Google’s February 2026 Core Update (specifically targeting Discover) has officially introduced a Local Layer that favors regional expertise over national generalists. I’ve been analyzing the early data from NewzDash, and the shift is undeniable:

📍 **Regional Personalization:** In state-level feeds (like NY and CA), local content volume jumped by up to 60%. Google is now effectively fencing Discover content to prioritize sites physically located in the user’s region.

🚫 **The End of Template Clickbait:** Low-value, mystery-style headlines from national aggregators are being suppressed.

💡 **Topic-by-Topic Authority:** Google isnt just looking at your domain; its looking at whether you are a regional expert in a specific niche (e.g., roofing, HVAC, law).

**The Takeaway:** For local businesses, the "moat" around your service area just got wider. If you produce high-utility, localized content, the algorithm is now structurally designed to give you a "home-field advantage" over national giants.
