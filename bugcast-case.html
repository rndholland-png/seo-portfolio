Predictive Engine Prototyping

BugCast

Transforming local meteorological data into proactive search authority triggers.

The Origin: Proactive Innovation & Grit

To capture the attention of a national pest control digital marketing agency, I didn't send a standard resume. Instead, I spent a weekend researching their operational inefficiencies and engineered a working programmatic solution.

Utilizing **Python, Google Colab, and AI-assisted "vibe coding,"** I developed BugCast—a predictive weather-driven SEO engine designed to align local search visibility with actual environmental pest surges. Even though the company missed out on the opportunity to connect, this prototype remains a powerful proof-of-concept demonstrating my drive to build high-value, data-driven solutions from scratch.

The Problem: Marketing Lags Behind Demand

  • ✕ Marketing Lags Behind Demand: Pest surges are entirely weather-driven (temperature spikes, sudden rainfall), but current content creation is always reactive.
  • ✕ Missed Visibility: Reactive Google Business Profile (GBP) posts and blog content miss the peak search window, costing local franchises high-value emergency leads.
  • ✕ Wasted Effort: Creative teams are forced to guess what to write about each week instead of executing based on verified, timely environmental data.

The Solution: BugCast Weather Alignment

  • ✓ PREDICT: Uses localized weather intelligence APIs to forecast specific insect and rodent surges 72 hours before they hit.
  • ✓ AUTOMATE: Instantly prompts content teams with timely, hyper-local briefs when risk spikes, ensuring perfect deployment timing.
  • ✓ PROFIT: Turns environmental forecasts into automated marketing triggers that surge client visibility, engagement, and direct phone leads.
