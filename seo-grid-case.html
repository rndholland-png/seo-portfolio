The SEO Grid

Bridging the gap between complex technical search data and creative content production.

The Problem & The Friction

Most content creators are brilliant writers, but they aren't technical SEO or Generative Engine (GEO) specialists. Traditional SEO briefs often overwhelm writers with raw keyword data, search volumes, and confusing syntax, leading to friction, missed deadlines, or improperly optimized content.

By designing The SEO Grid, we operationalized the handoff. Appended to the bottom of standard creative briefs, it acts as a clear, prescriptive "cheat sheet" that gives writers exactly what they need—from metadata to precise internal linking anchor text—without bogging them down in keyword theory.

The Outcome: Fostering Content-Dev Relationships

By presenting technical specifications in a standardized, easily copy-pasteable grid format, creative teams no longer look at SEO as a bottleneck or obstacle. This template saved countless hours in post-production rewrites, established seamless cross-team trust, and ensured that 100% of the published assets had proper technical schemas and keyword alignment out of the box.
