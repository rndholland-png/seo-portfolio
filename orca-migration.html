Migration Recovery

Orca Security Migration

Surgically restoring link equity and indexation safety flags within a critical 1-week post-launch window.

Timeframe 7 Days
Link Equity Retained 99.8%
Dev Collaboration Direct JIRA/Git

The Challenge

Following a rapid platform migration, critical structural issues emerged that threatened Orca Security's existing organic search footprint. Legacy high-authority backlinks were mapping to default 404 pages, and unintended search-exclusion directives began impacting key commercial pages. A swift intervention was required to coordinate with the Web Development team to protect brand equity.

Forensic Investigation & Diagnosis

Using a combination of server log analysis and crawl comparisons, I identified three primary structural failures:

  • Redirect Loop Cascade: Legacy redirect rules conflicted with the new routing architecture, trapping search engines in multi-hop redirect loops.
  • Indexation Blockers: Staging site noindex meta tags accidentally bypassed release filters and deployed directly to production.
  • Broken High-Value Backlinks: Crucial third-party editorial links pointing to outdated resource subdirectories were resolving to 404s instead of their closest semantic equivalents.

Surgical Execution

Rather than delivering a generic, massive PDF audit that developers would likely put on the back burner, I worked directly alongside the Engineering team inside their workflow:

Direct Ticket Integration

Translated SEO requirements into granular, action-oriented JIRA tasks, providing developers with exact rewrite rules, source-target paths, and expected server headers (200 OK, 301 Redirect).

Pre-deployment Crawl Verification

Executed isolated sandboxed crawls on hotfix branches to verify meta robots declarations, canonical targets, and redirect logic before changes went live to production.

The Outcome

By treating SEO technical issues with dev-level precision, we bypassed the typical post-migration dip. Indexation warnings in Google Search Console were cleared within 72 hours, 100% of the priority link-equity paths were successfully restored via single-hop permanent redirects, and organic visibility remained completely stable through the transition.
